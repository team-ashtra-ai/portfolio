Privacy Notice

Stay collects only the details a visitor chooses to submit through static forms. Form delivery currently uses Formspree and contact routing may also use public ASH-TRA links.

Analytics events are designed to avoid names, email addresses, phone numbers, messages, health details, financial details, legal details, and other personal form content.
